# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

Keylane welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experiences, abilities, identities, and levels of expertise.
- Keep feedback relevant and actionable. Respect boundaries and requests to stop.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, or private conversations without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and persistent disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock conversations, or limit participation when appropriate. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact; disagreement alone is not misconduct.

For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls to report privately to GitHub: [reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). This reaches GitHub, not a separate Keylane moderation team.

For project moderation that cannot be resolved in the existing conversation, you may open an issue asking the maintainer for a suitable contact channel **without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details**. A dedicated private project conduct-reporting channel has not yet been established. Do not post private allegations or personal evidence in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public conduct reports.

## 中文摘要

请尊重不同背景与能力的参与者，针对问题和代码提出建设性意见，不攻击个人。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视、泄露隐私以及持续干扰。维护者可根据影响删除内容、锁定讨论或限制参与。

GitHub 平台上的滥用可通过其「Report content / Report abuse」功能私密报告给 GitHub。项目尚未设立独立的私密行为举报渠道；公开请求维护者提供联系渠道时，不要披露当事人身份、私人指控或证据。安全漏洞请遵循安全策略。
